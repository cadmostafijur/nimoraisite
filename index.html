A few things to know.

Practical answers about projects, IDE access and the programme.

What can I explore with NIMORAI?

Token, NFT and coin project options, project preparation tools and access to a dedicated blockchain development IDE.

Do I need to know every technical detail before starting?

No. Start with your idea, then organise the identity, configuration and requirements step by step as your project brief takes shape.

How much does IDE access cost?

The NIMORAI IDE license is $700 USD as a one-time license purchase.

How do I receive IDE access?

Access instructions are delivered by email after confirmed payment and successful provisioning.

How does the affiliate programme work?

You receive a company-assigned coupon code to share in your content. When a customer applies your code during checkout, eligible commission is recorded for qualifying paid orders under the programme's configured rules.
